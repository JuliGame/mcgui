How the market works

Every listing is checked by staff before it goes up, and every coin goes through the server bank, so nobody can sell what they do not have. Prices are set by the sellers; the market only takes a small fee to keep the bank honest.

Buying is a click: the item lands in your ender chest, and the seller is paid the moment you take it out. If anything goes wrong, open a ticket and staff will put it right within the day.

Selling is just as easy. Hold the item, type the price, and it shows here within a minute. Listings last a week; anything unsold comes back to your ender chest.

Sell with us

Listings are free for the first week. After that the market takes one coin in every hundred, and nothing else.

Rules for sellers

Nothing duplicated, nothing griefed, nothing from another player's chest. Staff can see where every item came from, and a listing that breaks the rules is taken down with the seller's market rights.

Describe what you sell honestly. Enchantments, durability and how many are in a stack all go in the description. A listing that says less than it should is treated as one that lies.

The Market is run by the town council. Prices in coins; one coin is one gold nugget at the bank.
