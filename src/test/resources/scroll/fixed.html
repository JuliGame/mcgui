Server rulesRead them once; staff will assume you did.

1. Be kind

No harassment, slurs or threats, in chat, on signs or in builds. Disagree with the idea, not the person; staff decide what crosses the line.

2. No griefing

Do not break, take or change what is not yours, claimed or not. Unclaimed land near somebody's base is still theirs to the eye; ask first.

3. No cheating

No hacked clients, x-ray packs, duplication or auto-clickers. Minimaps and shaders are fine. If you are not sure, ask staff before you use it.

4. Keep chat readable

No spam, no walls of capitals, no advertising other servers. English in global chat, any language in private messages.

5. Trade fairly

Deliver what you sold, pay what you promised. Scams are griefing with extra steps, and are treated the same way.

6. Builds near spawn

Nothing within three hundred blocks of spawn without a plot from the town council; the council meets on Sundays at the town hall.

7. Redstone and lag

Clocks that never stop, huge mob farms and item lag machines are removed. Put a switch on what you build so staff can turn it off.

8. Accounts

One account per player. Shared accounts are banned together, and a ban follows the person to every account they play on.

9. Appeals

Banned? Appeal on the forum within thirty days, honestly. Appeals that blame the staff, the lag or a cousin are read last.

10. Staff have the last word

These rules cannot cover everything. When they do not, staff decide, and the decision stands until the council says otherwise.
