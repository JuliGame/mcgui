Server rules

Welcome to CraftHub. Breaking these rules gets you a warning, then a ban. Type /report <player> to tell staff, or read the FAQ.

  1. No griefing, stealing or killing players outside the arena.
  2. No hacked clients, x-ray packs or macros.
    • Minimaps are fine.
    • Auto-clickers are not.
  3. Be kind in chat. No spam, slurs or advertising.
